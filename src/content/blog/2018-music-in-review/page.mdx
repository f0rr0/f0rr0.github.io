As the year draws to an end, I must do my highly absurd annual music review. Apparently listening all year isn't enough; I also need to count it.

The count came to more than 7,300 plays and 28,379 minutes. That's considerably more time with music than last year. I remain quite good at finding opportunities to have something playing.

![Annual music plays from 2016 to 2018](./scrobbles.svg)

The more interesting number was 2,185 different tracks. Being exposed to new artists from Europe gave me plenty to explore, but a few tracks kept pulling me back. _Nobody Knows_ by Autograf became my all-time favourite with 225 listens. _Dawn's Insight_ by 2nd Exit reached number three with 200.

![Distinct tracks played each year from 2016 to 2018](./tracks.svg)

So there was both more wandering and more returning. I listened to 1,014 artists overall, and Allan Rayman won by a huge margin: fifteen hours of the year went to his music alone.

![Distinct artists played each year from 2016 to 2018](./artists.svg)

Spotify, meanwhile, told me I was listening to more obscure music than before. I am not sure if I should be worried or pleased. Only 34% fell within my most-listened genre, which seems about right for a year spent following one discovery into another.

![Spotify Wrapped's comparison of my listening with mainstream music](./obscure.png)

In the end, the figures bring me back to a couple of songs and an artist I kept choosing. Two hundred and twenty-five plays is a fairly committed way of saying I liked a track.
